About the store

From cost sheet
to shelf.

I started costing shoes at Clarks, scaled new manufacturing methods at Under Armour, and ran a Made in USA boot plant in Knoxville as Interim General Manager. Since then I've led supply chain, logistics, and planning programs at Adidas, Columbia Sportswear, and Nike.

That mix is the point. I know how a shoe should be made, what it should cost, and where it should be built. I also know the systems and business operations behind it, from PLM and planning tools to the processes that connect product, supply chain, and the rest of the business. Rain Consulting is where I bring all of that to brands.
